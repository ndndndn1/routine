# Multi-Fidelity Physics-Informed Neural Networks with Bayesian Uncertainty Quantification

- **arXiv**: [2602.01176](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01176)
- **Authors**: Olaf Yunus Laitinen Imanov
- **Submitted**: 2026-02-01
- **Keywords**: physics-informed, bayesian adaptive design, surrogate modeling, active learning

## Methods

1. **Multi-Fidelity BPINN (MF-BPINN)**: A hierarchical neural architecture that fuses cheap low-fidelity simulation data with sparse high-fidelity observations. Physics constraints (PDE residuals) are embedded in the loss function.

2. **Adaptive residual learning**: A learnable gating mechanism dynamically blends linear and nonlinear cross-fidelity discrepancy corrections, adapting to the complexity of the fidelity gap.

3. **Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (HMC) inference**: Full Bayesian treatment of network weights via HMC provides calibrated posterior uncertainty estimates over PDE solutions and parameters.

4. **Physics-informed loss**: PDE residual terms enforce conservation laws, boundary conditions, and initial conditions directly in the training objective, reducing data requirements.

5. **Parametric PDE focus**: Designed for settings requiring repeated solves over varying parameter configurations (e.g., material properties, forcing terms), where a single trained surrogate amortises the cost of many forward solves.

## Conclusion

MF-BPINN demonstrates that hierarchical multi-fidelity architectures with adaptive gating can effectively leverage inexpensive low-fidelity simulations to reduce reliance on expensive high-fidelity data. The Bayesian HMC framework provides rigorous, calibrated uncertainty estimates alongside PDE solutions, enabling reliable downstream decision-making in engineering applications.
