# GUIDe: Generative and Uncertainty-Informed Inverse Design for On-Demand Nonlinear Functional Responses

- **arXiv**: [2509.05641](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05641)
- **Authors**: Haoxuan Dylan Mu, Mingjian Tang, Wei Gao, Wei "Wayne" Chen
- **Submitted**: 2025-09-06 (revised 2025-10-08; published in Nature Communications 16, 1987)
- **Keywords**: surrogate modeling, active learning, bayesian optimization, multimodal inverse design, sequential design of experiments

## Methods

1. **Forward surrogate with UQ**: A data-driven forward model (design → nonlinear functional response) is trained with built-in uncertainty quantification. This replaces the intractable inverse mapping entirely.

2. **MCMC-based design sampling**: Markov Chain Monte Carlo samples the design space, weighting candidates by the forward model's confidence that the predicted response falls within a user-specified tolerance of the target.

3. **Uncertainty-informed feasibility scoring**: Each candidate design receives a probabilistic feasibility score based on the surrogate's confidence interval, naturally balancing exploration and exploitation.

4. **Diverse multimodal solutions**: The MCMC sampling inherently produces a diverse set of feasible designs, addressing the one-to-many ambiguity of inverse problems without requiring an explicit inverse model.

5. **Out-of-distribution robustness**: By working through a forward model rather than learning a direct inverse mapping, GUIDe avoids hallucinating infeasible designs for targets outside the training distribution.

6. **Validation**: Demonstrated on nacre-inspired composite materials with trilinear constitutive interface laws, targeting stress-strain curves. Benchmarked against genetic algorithm optimisation and conditional diffusion models.

## Conclusion

GUIDe consistently generates feasible, novel, and diverse inverse design solutions that outperform both genetic-algorithm-based and conditional diffusion model baselines, including for out-of-distribution targets. The forward-model-centric approach with uncertainty-informed MCMC sampling provides a model-agnostic and kernel-agnostic framework applicable to any engineering inverse problem with nonlinear functional responses.
